Testing Limitation — Navigation Refusing U-Turns
Vehicle navigation consistently refused to route U-turns independently. Only route-forced U-turns were executed, preventing a full and diverse sweep across regions. This resulted in repeated attempts at the same locations and fewer total U-turn opportunities than usual. Results may not be fully representative of build performance across all intersection types.

4-Lane Remains Strong
All 6 four-lane turns completed successfully (100%) across sessions 1 and 3. San Tomas corridor consistent. Slight wobble and fishhook noted but all S or M rated.

Early Turn Entry + Oversteer
This build consistently initiates U-turns early, cutting into the turn before the intersection center. Causes wobble and oversteer toward median curb requiring APTO (steering assist) at intersections where other builds performed normally. Noted across both 4-lane and 3-lane configurations.

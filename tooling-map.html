New-hire tooling primer

Six tools, one workflow — and DDB at the center.

You operate the car, record data, visualize it, browse it, and share what you build. DDB is the hub every other tool connects to; MagLev is the platform they live on. Start with the one-liners, then open the map to see how they wire together.

01 / The map

How the tools connect

DDB sits at the center — Occhio launches it, DriveScrubber links into it, DDBX packages workflows for it. MagLev is the platform that hosts them, and Kratos is the data platform on its edge. Select any tool to trace its connections.

Tap a tool — it turns amber, and everything it connects to turns blue. Notice Kratos connects only to MagLev — it replaces old data workflows and does not touch Occhio, DDB, or DriveScrubber.

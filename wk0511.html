Key Findings
sft-4461  ·  2026-04-30
Best overall U-turn trajectory quality. Turns were executed at optimal speed — quick but not aggressive — with tighter radii than subsequent builds. AMO-only drive demonstrated consistent completion. Hybrid drive revealed arbitration issues: classical interruptions degraded turn quality mid-maneuver and caused FP U-turn triggers (ghost brakes near median openings with BP flickering).
Best trajectory Tight & fast AMO Classical arbitration issues FP U-turn / ghost brake
PMB sft-4640  ·  2026-05-12
Baseline PMB build. Performed adequately on 3–4 lane configurations with acceptable pass rates, but struggled consistently on 2-lane and 2-lane + bike lane setups. Notable frequency of missed turns, fishhook maneuvers, and LK degradation events. No feasibility filter in place.
3–4 lane acceptable 2-lane high miss rate LK degradation Fishhook pattern
sft-4815 + Feasibility Filter  ·  2026-05-13 / 05-15
05/13 (filter refactored) showed improved 3–4 lane performance. 05/15 filter refactor did not behave as expected — system avoided narrower turns as if the refactor was inactive, consistent with the unrefactored filter profile. Both sessions still struggled with 2-lane + bike lane configurations.
3–4 lane improved 05/15 filter regression 2-lane + bike persistent Filter behavior TBD
🎯 DA Decision
R18 scope limited to 3–4 lane U-turns. 2-lane configurations (standard and + bike lane) deprioritized based on consistent failure rates across all builds and sessions.
